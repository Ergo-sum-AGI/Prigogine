# Stage 1.5 Concrete Instantiation — Sector-Analytic Nuclear Test Space and Continuity Checks

**Status:** Working mathematical draft for proof-obligation audit  
**Builds on:** `friedrichs_stage1_single_particle_foundation.md` (revision 2) and `friedrichs_stage1_5_nuclear_test_space.md`  
**Purpose:** Specify a concrete test-space realization $\Phi = \mathbb C|1\rangle \oplus \Phi_{\mathrm{cont}}$ for a standard class of form factors $V(\omega)$, and verify the core continuity, boundary-value, and domain-invariance requirements needed before Stage 2.

---

## 1. Reference form-factor class and deformation sector

Fix a deformation angle parameter $\theta_0 \in (0, \pi/2)$ and choose a deformation angle $\theta \in (0, \theta_0)$ as in Stage 1. Consider a sectorial domain
\[
\mathcal S_\delta = \left\{ z \in \mathbb C \setminus \{0\} : |\arg z| < \delta \right\},
\qquad \theta_0 < \delta < \frac{\pi}{2},
\]
and let $\overline{\mathcal S_\delta}$ denote its closure in $\mathbb C$ (including $0$).

### 1.1 Admissible form-factor assumptions ($V$)
Assume the coupling form factor $V$ satisfies:
1. **Holomorphy and reflection symmetry control:** $V$ is holomorphic on $\mathcal S_\delta$ and extends continuously to $\overline{\mathcal S_\delta} \setminus \{0\}$. Define $V^*(z) = \overline{V(\bar z)}$ on $\mathcal S_\delta$, so that on $\mathbb R^+$ we have $V^*(\omega) = \overline{V(\omega)}$ and $J(\omega) = V(\omega)V^*(\omega) = |V(\omega)|^2$.
2. **Threshold behavior:** Near $z = 0$ in $\overline{\mathcal S_\delta}$,
   \[
   |V(z)| \le C_0 |z|^\alpha, \qquad \alpha > 0,
   \]
   so $J(z) = O(|z|^{2\alpha})$ near $0$, matching the Stage 1 threshold assumption (11.1) (and covering the illustrative $\omega^{1/2}$ weight tail in Section 12 via $\alpha = 1/4$ or comparable $J$-scaling).
3. **High-energy decay:** On $\overline{\mathcal S_\delta}$, $V$ decays faster than any inverse power or exponentially at infinity, for example
   \[
   |V(z)| \le C_m (1 + |z|)^{-m} \quad \text{for every integer } m \ge 0,
   \qquad \text{or} \qquad
   |V(z)| \le C e^{-c |z|^\beta} \quad (c > 0,\ \beta > 0).
   \]
   In particular, $V \in L^2(\mathbb R^+)$ and the no-bound-state condition (1.5) can be satisfied for sufficiently small coupling $\lambda$ when $\omega_1 > 0$.

## 2. Concrete countably Hilbert nuclear test space $\Phi_{\mathrm{cont}}$

Let $\mathcal H_{\mathrm{cont}} = L^2(\mathbb R^+, d\omega)$. Define $\Phi_{\mathrm{cont}}$ as the space of functions $\psi : \overline{\mathcal S_\delta} \to \mathbb C$ such that:
- $\psi$ is holomorphic on the interior $\mathcal S_\delta$ and continuous on $\overline{\mathcal S_\delta}$;
- for every pair of integers $m, k \ge 0$, the seminorm
  \[
  \|\psi\|_{m,k}
  = \sup_{z \in \overline{\mathcal S_\delta}}
  \left( 1 + |z| \right)^m
  \left( 1 + |z|^{-1} \right)^k
  \left( |\psi(z)| + |\psi'(z)| \right)
  \]
  is finite.

Equip $\Phi_{\mathrm{cont}}$ with the locally convex topology generated by the countable family of norms $\{\|\cdot\|_{m,k}\}_{m,k \ge 0}$, and define the full test space
\[
\Phi = \mathbb C|1\rangle \oplus \Phi_{\mathrm{cont}}
\]
with the direct-sum topology (where $\mathbb C|1\rangle$ has the standard Euclidean/modulus norm).

### 2.1 Structural properties to verify in the proof audit
1. **Fréchet / nuclear structure:** Because the weights $(1+|z|)^m(1+|z|^{-1})^k$ grow faster than any polynomial at $\infty$ and near $0$ while derivative control $|\psi'| $ is included on $\overline{\mathcal S_\delta}$, compact embedding between norm completions (or standard weighted holomorphic/Gevrey nuclearity arguments) makes $\Phi_{\mathrm{cont}}$, and hence $\Phi$, a countably Hilbert nuclear space.
2. **Dense and continuous inclusion $\Phi \hookrightarrow \mathcal H$:** Restricting $\psi \in \Phi_{\mathrm{cont}}$ to $\mathbb R^+$ gives a function decaying rapidly at $\infty$ and bounded near $0$, hence in $L^2(\mathbb R^+)$, with
   \[
   \|\psi\|_{L^2(\mathbb R^+)}
   \le \left( \int_0^\infty \frac{d\omega}{(1+\omega)^4} \right)^{1/2} \|\psi\|_{2,0}.
   \]
   Thus the restriction map $\Phi \to \mathcal H$ is continuous, with dense range in $\mathcal H$.

## 3. Continuity of the Stage 1 generalized functionals on $\Phi$

Recall the test vector decomposition
\[
|\phi_\pm\rangle = c_\pm |1\rangle + \int_0^\infty \phi_\pm(\omega)|\omega\rangle\,d\omega,
\qquad \phi_-^*(z) = \overline{\phi_-(\bar z)}.
\]
Note that if $\phi_- \in \Phi_{\mathrm{cont}}$, then because $\overline{\mathcal S_\delta}$ is invariant under complex conjugation $z \mapsto \bar z$, the reflected map $\phi_-^*$ is also holomorphic on $\mathcal S_\delta$, continuous on $\overline{\mathcal S_\delta}$, and satisfies $\|\phi_-^*\|_{m,k} = \|\phi_-\|_{m,k}$.

### 3.1 Resonance functionals (Eqs. (6.2a)–(6.2b))
For a fixed resonance pole $z_0 \in \Sigma_\theta$ (so $z_0 \notin \Gamma_\theta$ and $\operatorname{dist}(z_0, \Gamma_\theta) = d_0 > 0$):
\[
\langle \phi_- | \psi_{z_0}^R \rangle
= \sqrt{A_1} \left[ \overline{c_-} + \lambda \int_{\Gamma_\theta} \frac{V(\zeta)\phi_-^*(\zeta)}{z_0 - \zeta}\,d\zeta \right],
\]
\[
\langle \psi_{z_0}^L | \phi_+ \rangle
= \sqrt{A_1} \left[ c_+ + \lambda \int_{\Gamma_\theta} \frac{\overline{V(\bar\zeta)}\phi_+(\zeta)}{z_0 - \zeta}\,d\zeta \right].
\]
Since $|z_0 - \zeta| \ge d_0 > 0$ on $\Gamma_\theta$ and $V$ is integrable along $\Gamma_\theta$ with rapid decay, we have the uniform norm estimates
\[
\left| \langle \phi_- | \psi_{z_0}^R \rangle \right|
\le |\sqrt{A_1}| \left( |c_-| + \frac{|\lambda|}{d_0} \left( \int_{\Gamma_\theta} |V(\zeta)|\,|d\zeta| \right) \|\phi_-\|_{0,0} \right),
\]
\[
\left| \langle \psi_{z_0}^L | \phi_+ \rangle \right|
\le |\sqrt{A_1}| \left( |c_+| + \frac{|\lambda|}{d_0} \left( \int_{\Gamma_\theta} |V(\bar\zeta)|\,|d\zeta| \right) \|\phi_+\|_{0,0} \right).
\]
Hence both resonance functionals are continuous linear functionals on $\Phi$.

### 3.2 Deformed-continuum functionals (Eqs. (7.4a)–(7.4b))
For $\zeta = x e^{-i\theta} \in \Gamma_\theta \setminus \{0\}$ ($x > 0$):
\[
\langle \phi_- | \psi_\zeta^R \rangle
= \phi_-^*(\zeta) + \frac{\lambda \overline{V(\bar\zeta)}}{\eta_-(\zeta)} \left[ \overline{c_-} + \lambda \int_{\Gamma_\theta} \frac{V(\zeta')\phi_-^*(\zeta')}{\zeta - \zeta' - i0_\theta}\,d\zeta' \right],
\]
\[
\langle \psi_\zeta^L | \phi_+ \rangle
= \phi_+(\zeta) + \frac{\lambda V(\zeta)}{\eta_+(\zeta)} \left[ c_+ + \lambda \int_{\Gamma_\theta} \frac{\overline{V(\bar\zeta')}\phi_+(\zeta')}{\zeta - \zeta' + i0_\theta}\,d\zeta' \right].
\]
For any fixed $\zeta \neq 0$ on $\Gamma_\theta$ (away from any zeros of $\eta_\mp(\zeta)$):
1. Point evaluation $\phi_-^*(\zeta)$ and $\phi_+(\zeta)$ are bounded by $\|\phi_\mp\|_{0,0}$.
2. Because $\phi_\mp^*$ and $\phi_+$ are holomorphic on a neighborhood of $\Gamma_\theta$ in $\mathcal S_\delta$ and decay rapidly at $\infty$, the principal-value / boundary Cauchy integral along $\Gamma_\theta$ is bounded in terms of $\|\phi_\mp\|_{m,k}$ (using local Cauchy/Hölder control of $\phi_\mp^*$ or $\phi_+$ near $\zeta'$ and rapid decay at $\infty$).
3. Therefore $\phi_- \mapsto \langle \phi_- | \psi_\zeta^R \rangle$ and $\phi_+ \mapsto \langle \psi_\zeta^L | \phi_+ \rangle$ are continuous on $\Phi$ for each fixed $\zeta \in \Gamma_\theta \setminus \{0\}$.

## 4. Domain invariance and distributional checks

1. **Action of $H$ on $\Phi$:** On $\Phi_{\mathrm{cont}}$, multiplication by $\omega$ maps $\psi(z) \mapsto z\psi(z)$, which preserves holomorphy on $\mathcal S_\delta$, continuity on $\overline{\mathcal S_\delta}$, and finiteness of $\|\cdot\|_{m,k}$ (shifting the decay index $m$ by $1$ and threshold index $k$ by $-1$ for $k \ge 1$). Adding $\lambda V(z) c_+$ preserves $\Phi_{\mathrm{cont}}$ under the form-factor assumptions of Section 1.1. Thus $\Phi$ is invariant under $H$, supporting the algebraic reductions (8.3)–(8.6) on test vectors.
2. **Boundary limits and jump factorization (7.3):** With $\phi_\pm \in \Phi_{\mathrm{cont}}$ holomorphic across $\Gamma_\theta$ and rapidly decaying, the boundary limits $\pm i0_\theta$ along $\Gamma_\theta$ commute with the contour integrations in the four resolvent blocks of (2.1), allowing the Sokhotski–Plemelj identity (7.2) and jump identity (7.5) to be applied cleanly in `To establish the continuum resolvent jump.txt`.
3. **Remaining analytic/asymptotic items:** Establishing the exact sectorial analytic continuation of $\eta_+(z)$ to $\Sigma_\theta$, proving that no non-real/embedded eigenvalues or unwanted singularities obstruct contour deformation (5.4), and proving the dominated-convergence bound as $z = iy \to i\infty$ for completeness (9.1) remain explicit proof obligations to be discharged with the chosen form factor $V$.
